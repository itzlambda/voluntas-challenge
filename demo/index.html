How it's built

Markets are discovered from Gamma and lifecycle sockets, subscribed twice across two WebSocket pools, routed to a single owning worker, and encoded by one writer thread.

every market is subscribed in both pools

Why multiple workers?

I planned to move SBE encoding into the workers, but that required changing the supplied encoding path. I kept encoding in the writer.

Markets are independent, yet current CPU usage suggests one book worker may suffice. The pool may be premature optimization. I kept it due to time constraints.

Workers are schematic. Socket counts show configured values until live metrics arrive.

Ownership rules

  • A market stays with one worker for the life of the process
  • Only that worker reads or changes its books
  • Only the writer touches the output file
  • Connections hold no book state
  • Pools only keep a market's two copies on different sockets
