Order-book sockets

Traffic on each upstream WebSocket. Select a socket to see its history, its markets, and their live order books.

Decoded market-data events per second on each upstream order-book socket, primary and replica, over a one-minute rate. Full books count, so a reconnect's burst of full books raises the rate for about a minute. Heartbeats and control messages do not count. There are no per-market rates.

The distribution includes connected sockets with markets and a rate. Exactly zero has its own bar. Sockets that are disconnected, have no markets, are warming up or have no current rate are counted separately and never shown as zero.

Bins are fitted to the rates when the page loads and stay fixed between refreshes so bars stay comparable. Rates far outside the fitted range go to the end bars. Use Fit range to refit.

Socket telemetry is scraped every 15 seconds. A rate needs four samples, so it appears about a minute after a new socket task starts or the collector restarts. Reconnects keep the counter, and is shown only while the socket keeps publishing fresh telemetry. History loads for the selected socket only; gaps mean no rate, and dashed lines mark collector restarts, after which the same ID may carry different markets.
